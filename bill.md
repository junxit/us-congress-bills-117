# H.R. 8515

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to allow for the furnishing of audio-only telehealth services.

## § 1. Allowing for the furnishing of audio-only telehealth services

Section 1834(m)(9) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395m(m)(9)) is amended by striking “during the 151-day period beginning on” and inserting “on or after”.

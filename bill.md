# H.R. 5286

> To establish a Chinese Corporate Human Rights Abusers List, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents; purpose

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Protecting Americans from Corporate Human Rights Abusers Act”.
- **(b)** *Table of contents* The table of contents for this Act is as follows:
- **(c)** *Purpose* The purpose of this Act is to codify into law the provisions of Executive Order 14032 of June 3, 2021 (86 Fed. Reg. 30145; relating to Addressing the Threat From Securities Investments That Finance Certain Companies of the People’s Republic of China), as in effect on the date of the enactment of this Act.

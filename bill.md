# H.R. 6044

> To amend the Post-Katrina Emergency Management Reform Act of 2006 to extend the authorization of the Emergency Management Performance Grants Program through 2026.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “FEMA Emergency Management Performance Grants Program Reauthorization Act of 2021”.

## § 2. Authorization of emergency management performance grants

Section 662(f) of the Post-Katrina Emergency Management Reform Act of 2006 (6 U.S.C. 762(f)) is amended by striking “2018 through 2022” and inserting “2022 through 2026”.

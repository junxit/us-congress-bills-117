# S. 4798

> To rescind certain funding provided to the Internal Revenue Service under section 10301 of Public Law 117–169.

## § 1. Rescission of certain funds for enhanced Internal Revenue Service resources

Effective on the date of enactment of this Act, the unobligated balances of the amounts made available under the following provisions of Public Law 117–169 are rescinded:

- **(1)** *Internal Revenue Service enforcement funds* Section 10301(1)(A)(ii).
- **(2)** *Internal Revenue Service operations support* Section 10301(1)(A)(iii).

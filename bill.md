# H.R. 8348

> To provide it is the policy of the United States that the NATO-Russia Founding Act, signed May 27, 1997, in Paris, does not constrain the deployment of United States or NATO forces in any way.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “United States NATO Force Deployment Policy Act of 2022”.

## § 2. Statement of policy

It is the policy of the United States that the NATO-Russia Founding Act, signed May 27, 1997, in Paris, does not constrain the deployment of United States or NATO forces in any way.

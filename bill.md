# H.R. 6098

> To amend the American Rescue Plan Act of 2021 to provide reimbursements for licensing and exam fees under the Veteran Rapid Retraining Assistance Program of the Department of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Reimbursements for licensing and exam fees under Veteran Rapid Retraining Assistance Program

Subsection (c) of section 8006 of the American Rescue Plan Act of 2021 (Public Law 117–2; 38 U.S.C. 3001 note prec.) is amended by adding at the end the following new paragraph:

> - **(5)** *Licensing and exam fees* In carrying out retraining assistance under this section, the Secretary shall provide reimbursements to eligible veterans for licensing and exam fees in a manner the Secretary determines appropriate.

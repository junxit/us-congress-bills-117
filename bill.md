# H.J. RES. 86

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of a rule submitted by the Department of Justice relating to “Definition of “Frame or Receiver” and Identification of Firearms”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Justice relating to “Definition of “Frame or Receiver” and Identification of Firearms” (published at 87 Fed. Reg. 24652 (April 26, 2022)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.

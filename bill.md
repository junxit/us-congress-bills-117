# S. 787

> To amend the Atchafalaya National Heritage Area Act to extend the authority of the Secretary of the Interior to provide assistance to the local coordinating entity for the Atchafalaya National Heritage Area under that Act.

## § 1. Extension of authority for the Atchafalaya National Heritage Area

Section 221 of the Atchafalaya National Heritage Area Act (54 U.S.C. 320101 note; Public Law 109–338; 120 Stat. 1795) is amended by striking “15 years” and inserting “30 years”.

# S. RES. 132

> Expressing the sense of the Senate that the current influx of migrants is causing a crisis at the Southern border.

## Preamble

- Now, therefore, be it

That it is the sense of the Senate that the current influx of migrants at the Southern land border of the United States constitutes a crisis.

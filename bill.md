# H.R. 8523

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to require a State receiving a block grant for prevention and treatment of substance use disorders to describe the State’s efforts to encourage and promote work and employment to improve mental health and well-being.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Promote Work and Improve Health Act of 2022”.

## § 2. Description of the State efforts to promote work and employment to improve mental health and well-being

Section 1932(b)(1)(A) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300x(b)(1)(A)) is amended—

- **(1)** in clause (viii), by striking “and” at the end;
- **(2)** in clause (ix), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(x)** provides a description of the State’s efforts to encourage and promote work and employment to improve mental health and well-being.

# H.R. 9030

> To repeal the corporate alternative minimum tax.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Repealing the Ill-Conceived and Problematic (RIP) Book Minimum Tax Act ”.

## § 2. Repeal of corporate alternative minimum tax

- **(a)** *In general* Section 10101 and section 13904(a) of Public Law 117–169 are repealed.
- **(b)** *Applicability* The Internal Revenue Code of 1986 shall be applied and administered as if such sections (and the amendments made by such sections) had not been enacted.

# H.R. 5731

> To provide that no Federal funds may be expended to implement certain law enforcement partnerships, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Championing our Parents Act of 2021”.

## § 2. Prohibition on use of Federal funds

No Federal funds may be expended to implement the memorandum from the Attorney General entitled “Partnership among Federal, State, Local, Tribal, and Territorial Law Enforcement to address threats against School Administrators, Board Members, Teachers, and Staff” dated October 4, 2021.

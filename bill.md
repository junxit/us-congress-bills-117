# H.R. 5400

> To amend the Universal Service Antideficiency Temporary Suspension Act to further exempt the Universal Service Fund from certain title 31 provisions.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Preventing Disruptions to Universal Service Funds Act”.

## § 2. Application of certain title 31 provisions to Universal Service Fund

Section 302 of the Universal Service Antideficiency Temporary Suspension Act (Public Law 108–494; 118 Stat. 3998) is amended by striking “December 31, 2021” each place it appears and inserting “December 31, 2024”.

# H.R. 4221

> To direct the United States Postal Service to designate 77416 as the single, unique ZIP Code for Sargent, Texas, and for other purposes.

## § 1. ZIP Code for Sargent, Texas

Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the United States Postal Service shall designate 77416 as the single, unique ZIP Code applicable to Sargent, Texas.

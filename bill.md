# S. RES. 128

> Notifying the House of Representatives of the election of a Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate.

That the House of Representatives be notified of the election of the Honorable Karen H. Gibson as Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate.

# S. 4774

> To amend the Clean Air Act to modify the ethanol waiver for Reid vapor pressure limitations, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ethanol Access for a Sustainable Economy Act of 2022” or the “EASE Act of 2022”.

## § 2. Ethanol waiver

Section 211(h)(4) of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7545(h)(4)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), by inserting “or more of” after “10 percent”; and
- **(2)** in subparagraph (C), by striking “additional alcohol or”.

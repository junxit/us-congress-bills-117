# H. RES. 962

> Supporting the people of America.

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** demands an immediate border wall be constructed on the southern border;
- **(2)** supports, unequivocally, America’s sovereignty and territorial integrity;
- **(3)** urges United States allies and partners to deliver additional and immediate defensive security assistance to help the United States address illegal border crossings;
- **(4)** commits to continuing to support U.S. Customs and Border Protection and U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement;
- **(5)** reaffirms its strong support for the security of the United States;
- **(6)** pledges to bolster United States energy security and reduce its dependence on Russian energy imports;
- **(7)** underscores the importance of maintaining United States energy independence for the benefit of the American people;
- **(8)** supports the right of the American people to protest, and demands the immediate release of all those who have been unjustly detained; and
- **(9)** stands steadfastly, staunchly, proudly, and fervently behind the American people in their fight for the preservation of the Republic.

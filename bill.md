# H.J. RES. 97

> Disapproving of the rule submitted by the Department of Homeland Security relating to “Public Charge Ground of Inadmissibility”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Homeland Security relating to “Public Charge Ground of Inadmissibility” (87 Fed. Reg. 5547), and such rule shall have no force or effect.

# H.R. 7832

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 396 South California Avenue in West Covina, California, as the “Esteban E. Torres Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Esteban E. Torres Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 396 South California Avenue in West Covina, California, shall be known and designated as the “Esteban E. Torres Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Esteban E. Torres Post Office Building”.

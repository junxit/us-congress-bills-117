# S. 1329

> To amend the National Aviation Heritage Area Act to reauthorize the National Aviation Heritage Area, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “National Aviation Heritage Area Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of the National Aviation Heritage Area

Section 512 of the National Aviation Heritage Area Act (54 U.S.C. 320101 note; Public Law 108–447; 118 Stat. 3367; 133 Stat. 2713) is amended by striking “September 30, 2022” and inserting “September 30, 2036”.

# S. 3514

> To repeal COVID–19 vaccination requirements imposed by the District of Columbia.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Restore Parental and Consumer Medical Rights in the Nation's Capital Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of DC vaccine mandates

The following shall have no force or effect:

- **(1)** Order 2021–148 issued by the Mayor of the District of Columbia on December 22, 2021.
- **(2)** The Coronavirus Immunization of School Students and Early Childhood Workers Amendment Act of 2021 (D.C. Act 24–285), enacted by the District of Columbia Council on January 12, 2022.

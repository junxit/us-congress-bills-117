# H.R. 9483

> To amend the Agricultural Foreign Investment Disclosure Act of 1978 with respect to certain agricultural land transactions, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Agriculture Foreign Investment Transparency Act”.

## § 2. Requirements relating to certain agricultural land transactions

- **(a)** *Reporting requirement* Section 2 of the Agricultural Foreign Investment Disclosure Act of 1978 (7 U.S.C. 3501) is amended by striking “, other than a security interest,” each place it appears.
- **(b)** *Public inspection* Section 7 of the Agricultural Foreign Investment Disclosure Act of 1978 (7 U.S.C. 3506) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “Any report” and inserting the following:
    > - **(a)** *In general* Any report

  - **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(b)** *Public website*
    >   - **(1)** *In general* Not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this subsection, the Secretary shall make publicly available on the website of the Department each report submitted to the Secretary under section 2 not later than 30 days after the date on which such report is received by the Secretary.
    >   - **(2)** *Past reports* Not later than 1 year after the date of the enactment of this subsection, the Secretary shall make publicly available on the website of the Department each report submitted to the Secretary on or after January 1, 2000.

- **(c)** *Definitions* Section 9 of the Agricultural Foreign Investment Disclosure Act of 1978 (7 U.S.C. 3508) is amended—
  - **(1)** by redesignating paragraphs (4), (5), and (6) as paragraphs (5), (6), and (7), respectively;
  - **(2)** in paragraph (1)—
    - **(A)** by striking “States and used” and inserting
      > States that—
      > - **(A)** is used

    - **(B)** by adding “or” after the semicolon; and
    - **(C)** by adding at the end the following:
      > - **(B)** in the preceding 10-year period, was used for a purpose described in subparagraph (A);

  - **(3)** in paragraph (3)—
    - **(A)** in subparagraph (C), by striking “and” at the end;
    - **(B)** by redesignating subparagraph (D) as subparagraph (E); and
    - **(C)** by inserting after subparagraph (C) the following:
      > - **(D)** any person, other than an individual or a government, that issues equity securities that are primarily traded on a foreign securities exchange; and

  - **(4)** by inserting after paragraph (3) the following:
    > - **(4)** the term “interest” includes—
    >   - **(A)** a security interest; and
    >   - **(B)** a lease, without regard to duration of term;

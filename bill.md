# H.R. 5558

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to reauthorize preventive health measures with respect to prostate cancer, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Prostate Cancer Prevention Act”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of preventive health measures with respect to prostate cancer

Section 317D(l)(1) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 247b–5(l)(1)) is amended by striking “$20,000,000 for fiscal year 1993, and such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 1994 through 2004” and inserting “$14,205,000 for each of fiscal years 2022 through 2026”.

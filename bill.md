# H.J. RES. 88

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Securities and Exchange Commission related to “The Enhancement and Standardization of Climate-Related Disclosures for Investors”.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Securities and Exchange Commission relating to “The Enhancement and Standardization of Climate-Related Disclosures for Investors” (___ Fed. Reg. ____ (____ __, ____)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.

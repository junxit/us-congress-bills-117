# H.R. 8875

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to expand eligibility of members of the National Guard for housing loans guaranteed by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Expanding Home Loans for Guard and Reservists Act”.

## § 2. Expansion of eligibility of members of the National Guard for housing loans guaranteed by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs

Section 3701(b)(7) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “full-time National Guard duty” and inserting “active service”.

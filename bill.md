# H.R. 2338

> To require the commanding general of the District of Columbia National Guard to reside in the District of Columbia.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “District of Columbia National Guard Commanding General Residency Act”.

## § 2. Requirement that the commanding general of the District of Columbia National Guard reside in the District of Columbia

- **(a)** *Requirement* The commanding general of the District of Columbia National Guard shall reside in the District of Columbia.
- **(b)** *Effective date* Subsection (a) shall take effect on the day that is one year after the date of the enactment of this Act.

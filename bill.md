# H.R. 6382

> To nullify the order of the Mayor of the District of Columbia imposing a vaccine entry requirement for certain establishments and facilities, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Nullification of vaccine entry requirement of District of Columbia

Order 2021–148 of the Mayor of the District of Columbia (December 22, 2021) and any successor or substantially similar order of the Mayor of the District of Columbia shall have no force or effect.

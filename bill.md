# S. 3305

> To repeal the amendment made to the Superfund excise taxes by the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Chemical Tax Repeal Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of certain Superfund excise tax modifications

The amendments made by section 80201 of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act are repealed.

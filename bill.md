# H.R. 5987

> To amend the Toxic Substances Control Act with respect to the PFAS data call, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “PFAS Definition Improvement Act”.

## § 2. PFAS data call

Section 8(a)(7) of the Toxic Substances Control Act (15 U.S.C. 2607(a)(7)) is amended by inserting “that contains at least one fully fluorinated carbon atom,” after “perfluoroalkyl or polyfluoroalkyl substance”.

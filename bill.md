# H.R. 6277

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude ethylene from taxation under the Superfund excise tax.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ethylene is Essential for Chemistry and Manufacturing Act”.

## § 2. Excluding ethylene from taxation under Superfund excise tax

- **(a)** *In general* The table contained in section 4661(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking the row relating to ethylene.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall take effect on July 1, 2022.

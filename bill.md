# H.R. 811

> To require all individuals in the executive branch to comply with Executive orders imposing mandates on the people of the United States.

## § 1. Executive branch compliance with executive order public mandates

- **(a)** *In general* The President, Vice President, and all officers and employees in the executive branch of the Federal Government shall comply with each Executive order that imposes a mandate on the people of the United States.
- **(b)** *Uniformed services excluded* Subsection (a) shall not apply with respect to members of the uniformed services.

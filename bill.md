# H.R. 8082

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 101 West Washington Street in Charles Town, West Virginia, as the “Corporal Frank Buckles Post Office”.

## § 1. Corporal Frank Buckles Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 101 West Washington Street in Charles Town, West Virginia, shall be known and designated as the “Corporal Frank Buckles Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Corporal Frank Buckles Post Office”.

# H.R. 4178

> To extend the authority to collect Shasta-Trinity Marina fees through fiscal year 2027.

## § 1. Shasta-Trinity Marina fees

Section 422 of the Department of the Interior, Environment, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2008 (Public Law 110–161; 121 Stat 2149) as amended, is further amended by striking “and each subsequent fiscal year through fiscal year 2019” and inserting “and each subsequent fiscal year through fiscal year 2027”.

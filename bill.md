# H.R. 7809

> To amend the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 to provide for law enforcement officer bonuses.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Communities Deserve Cops Act”.

## § 2. Law Enforcement Officer Bonuses

Subsection (b) of section 1701 of title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (34 U.S.C. 10381) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (22), by striking “and” at the end;
- **(2)** in paragraph (23), by striking the period at the end and inserting a semicolon; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(24)** to provide a signing bonus for a law enforcement officer in an amount not to exceed $5,000; and
  > - **(25)** to provide a retention bonus for a law enforcement officer in an amount not to exceed $5,000.

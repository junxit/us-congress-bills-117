# H.R. 1310

> To authorize additional district judgeships for the district of Colorado, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Colorado Judgeship Act”.

## § 2. Additional district judgeships for the district of Colorado

- **(a)** *Additional district judgeships* The President shall appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, 3 additional district judges for the district of Colorado.

# H.R. 6489

> To prohibit the use of certain grants from being used to procure unmanned aircraft systems manufactured by Chinese companies, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Infrastructure Grant Security Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on acquisition or operation of Chinese manufactured UAS using SMART grant funds

Section 25005(e)(3) of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (Public Law 117–58) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (B) by striking “or” at the end;
- **(2)** in subparagraph (C) by striking the period at the end and inserting “; or”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(D)** to purchase, lease, or operate an unmanned aircraft system (as defined in section 44801 of title 49, United States Code) manufactured by—
  >   - **(i)** an entity domiciled in the People’s Republic of China; or
  >   - **(ii)** an entity, or a subsidiary or affiliate of an entity, that is subject to influence or control by—
  >     - **(I)** the Government of the People’s Republic of China; or
  >     - **(II)** the Chinese Communist Party.

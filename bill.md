# S. 2466

> To reform the Federal sugar program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fair Sugar Policy Act of 2021”.

## § 2. Sugar program

Section 156(a) of the Federal Agriculture Improvement and Reform Act of 1996 (7 U.S.C. 7272(a)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (4), by striking “and” at the end; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (5), by striking “2019 through 2023 crop years.” and inserting the following: “2019 and 2020 crop years; and
  > - **(6)** 18.75 cents per pound for raw cane sugar for each of the 2021 through 2025 crop years.

# H.R. 8308

> To require the Secretary of the Air Force to submit a report to the Committee on Armed Services of the House of Representatives on a plan to characterize the asbestos risk of former Air Force defense sites, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Report on plan to characterize asbestos risk of former Air Force defense sites

- **(a)** *Report* Not later than December 15, 2022, the Secretary of the Air Force shall develop and submit to the Committee on Armed Services of the House of Representatives a report on a plan to characterize the asbestos risk of defense sites formerly used by or under the jurisdiction of the Secretary and, if required, provide remediation for such sites.
- **(b)** *Matters included* The report under subsection (a) shall include, at a minimum—
  - **(1)** a list of defense sites formerly used by or under the jurisdiction of the Secretary with known or expected asbestos contamination;
  - **(2)** a timeline and prioritized list for characterizing the asbestos risk of such sites through site inspections and remedial investigations; and
  - **(3)** an assessment of the deterioration of facilities and the corresponding risk of friable asbestos being released into soils and groundwater.

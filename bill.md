# H.R. 4229

> To reauthorize grants for the Rape, Abuse and Incest National Network, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “National Sexual Assault Hotline Grants Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of grants for the RAINN national sexual assault hotline and the RAINN national sexual assault online hotline

Section 628(d) of the Adam Walsh Child Protection and Safety Act of 2006 (34 U.S.C. 20985(d)) is amended by striking “fiscal years 2007 through 2010” and inserting “fiscal years 2022 through 2028”.

# H.R. 1513

> To require that all Special Drawing Rights allocations be authorized by Congress in law.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Special Drawing Rights Oversight Act”.

## § 2. Congressional approval required for all allocations of Special Drawing Rights

Section 6(a) of the Special Drawing Rights Act (22 U.S.C. 286q(a)) is amended by striking “so that allocations” and all that follows and inserting a period.

# H. RES. 1468

> Of inquiry directing the Director of National Intelligence to transmit certain documents to the House of Representatives relating to the investigation by the intelligence community into the origins of the COVID–19 virus.

That the Director of National Intelligence is directed to transmit to the House of Representatives, not later than 14 days after the date of the adoption of this resolution, copies of all documents (including records, memos, correspondence, and other communications), to the extent that any such one or more items are within the possession of the Director and refer or relate to the investigation by the intelligence community into the origins of the COVID–19 virus.

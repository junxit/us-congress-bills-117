# H.R. 9238

> To transfer certain amounts authorized to be appropriated for foreign assistance to the Department of Homeland Security for programs securing the southern border of the United States, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Border Patrol First Act”.

## § 2. Transfer required

Of the unobligated balances of amounts made available to the Department of State for refugee and migration assistance and for humanitarian assistance in Central America, $240,000,000 shall be transferred to and merged with amounts made available to the Department of Homeland Security—

- **(1)** for the programs, projects, and activities of U.S. Customs and Border Protection along the southern international border of the United States; and
- **(2)** to reimburse the States of Texas and Arizona for programs, projects, or activities conducted by such States relating to securing the southern international border of the United States.

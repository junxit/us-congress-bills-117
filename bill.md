# H.R. 9694

> For the relief of Jan van Hoek.

## § 1. Exemption of Jan van Hoek from National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program petition deadlines

The deadlines described in section 2116 of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300aa–16) with respect to petitions filed for compensation under the National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program shall not apply to a petition filed with respect to a vaccine-related injury occurring as a result of the administration to Jan van Hoek of a vaccine during the period that began on March 6, 2007, and ended on December 31, 2010.

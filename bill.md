# H. RES. 1026

> Expressing the sense of the House of Representatives that any Member of the House who makes a public statement advocating for defunding the police should not be permitted to use Federal funds to contract with a private entity for security personnel support, and for other purposes.

That—

- **(1)** the House of Representatives condemns any calls made by a Member of the House to “defund” the police; and
- **(2)** it is the sense of the House that any Member of the House who makes a public statement advocating for, with respect to any State or local jurisdiction, abolishing or disbanding a police department or significantly reducing the budget of a police department should not be permitted to use any Federal funds, including amounts in the Members’ Representational Allowance, to contract with a private entity for security personnel support.

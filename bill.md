# H.R. 8981

> To streamline hardrock mine permitting on Federal lands, support technological and scientific advancements for mineral development, expand the mining workforce, track global supply chains, strengthen domestic refining and processing capacity, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Securing America’s Mineral Supply Chains Act of 2022”.

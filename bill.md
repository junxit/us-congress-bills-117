# H.R. 5383

> To amend the Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007 to reauthorize the Smart Grid Investment Matching Grant Program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Grid Hardening Act of 2021”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of Smart Grid Investment Matching Grant Program

Section 1306 of the Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007 (42 U.S.C. 17386) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (d), by redesignating paragraph (9) as paragraph (10) and inserting after paragraph (8) the following:
  > - **(9)** The ability to use technology, equipment, or hardening measures to enable the electric grid to better withstand the effects of extreme weather, a wildfire, or any other natural disaster.

- **(2)** in subsection (f), by striking “2008 through 2012” and inserting “2022 through 2026”.

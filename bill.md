# S. 2758

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to impose an excise tax on stock buybacks of publicly traded corporations.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stock Buyback Accountability Act of 2021”.

- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to repurchases (within the meaning of section 4501(c) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as added by this section) of stock after December 31, 2021.

# S. CON. RES. 45

> Providing for a correction in the enrollment of H.R. 6833.

That, in the enrollment of H.R. 6833, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall amend the title so as to read: “Making continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2023, and for other purposes.”.

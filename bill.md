# S. 2248

> To increase the penalties for making personal information about a Federal law enforcement officer or other Federal officer available to the public.

## § 1. Protecting Federal law enforcement officers from doxing

Section 119(a) of title 18, United States Code, is amended, in the undesignated matter following paragraph (2), by striking “5 years” and inserting “10 years”.

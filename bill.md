# S. 1431

> To prohibit the Director of the Office of Management and Budget from raising the minimum urban area population to qualify a metropolitan statistical area from 50,000, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Metropolitan Statistical Area Stabilization Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition and study on metropolitan statistical area designation

The Director of the Office of Management and Budget shall not raise the minimum urban area population to qualify a metropolitan statistical area from 50,000.

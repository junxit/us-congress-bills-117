# H.R. 659

> To repeal the limitation on the construction of new public housing, known as the Faircloth Amendment, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Repeal the Faircloth Amendment Act of 2021”.

## § 2. Repeal of Faircloth amendment

Section 9(g) of the United States Housing Act of 1937 (42 U.S.C. 1437g(g)) is amended by striking paragraph (3) (relating to limitation on new construction).

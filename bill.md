# H.R. 7154

> To require the President to report to Congress on authorities relating to the stabilization and reduction of gas prices, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “He Did That Act”.

## § 2. Report on authorities relating to gas prices

Not later than 30 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the President shall submit to Congress a report that identifies existing authorities that the President may use to help stabilize and reduce gas prices.

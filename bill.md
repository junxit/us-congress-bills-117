# H.R. 5169

> To improve care furnished and to support the workforce in skilled nursing facilities under the Medicare program and in nursing facilities under the Medicaid program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Nursing Home Improvement and Accountability Act of 2021”.

# S. 1647

> To authorize an exception to the restriction on construction of Coast Guard vessels in foreign shipyards for certain construction in shipyards in North Atlantic Treaty Organization countries.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Enhancing Coast Guard Readiness Act”.

## § 2. Exception to restriction on construction of Coast Guard vessels in foreign shipyards for certain construction in shipyards in North Atlantic Treaty Organization countries

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1151 of title 14, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (a), by striking “subsection (b)” and inserting “subsections (b) and (c)”; and
  - **(2)** by adding at the end the following new subsection:
    > - **(c)** Subsection (a) shall not apply with respect to construction otherwise covered by that subsection if—
    >   - **(1)** the foreign shipyard concerned is located in a North Atlantic Treaty Organization country; and
    >   - **(2)** the cost of the construction concerned is less than the cost would be if such construction occurred in a domestic shipyard.

- **(b)** *Conforming amendment* Section 8679(a) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by inserting “and section 1151(c) of title 14” after “in subsection (b)”.

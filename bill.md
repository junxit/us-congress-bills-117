# H.R. 7523

> To repeal the American Servicemembers’ Protection Act of 2002.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Repeal Hague Invasion Act”.

## § 2. Repeal

The American Servicemembers’ Protection Act of 2002 (title II of Public Law 107–206; 22 U.S.C. 7421 et seq.) is repealed.

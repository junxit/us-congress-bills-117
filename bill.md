# H.R. 5935

> To prohibit adverse action against a member of the Armed Forces solely on the basis that such member refuses to receive a vaccination against COVID-19 or disclose whether such member has received such a vaccination.

## § 1. Prohibition of adverse action against a member of the Armed Forces solely on the basis that such member refuses to receive a vaccination against COVID-19 or disclose whether such member has received such a vaccination

The Secretary concerned (as that term is defined in section 101 of title 10, United States Code) may not take any adverse action against a member of the Armed Forces solely on the basis that such member refuses—

- **(1)** to receive a vaccination against COVID-19; or
- **(2)** disclose whether such member has received such a vaccination.

# H.R. 8066

> To make technical changes clarifying section 502 of the Federal Credit Reform Act of 1990.

## § 1. Technical changes with respect to definition of direct loan

Section 502(1) of the Federal Credit Reform Act of 1990 (2 U.S.C. 661a(1)) is amended by striking “or the price support loans of the Commodity Credit Corporation” and inserting “, the price support loans of the Commodity Credit Corporation, or a loan made by another lender or financing arrangements that defer payment for 90 days or fewer”.

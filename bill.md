# S. 4826

> To amend the Healthy Forests Restoration Act of 2003 to modify the definition of the term “at-risk community”.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Community Wildfire Protection Act of 2022”.

## § 2. Definition of at-risk community

Section 101(1) of the Healthy Forests Restoration Act of 2003 (16 U.S.C. 6511(1)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking subparagraph (A); and
- **(2)** by redesignating subparagraphs (B) and (C) as subparagraphs (A) and (B), respectively.

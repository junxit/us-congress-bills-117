# H.R. 7807

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to expand eligibility for, and extend authorization of, certain programs for homeless veterans.

## § 1. Health Care for Homeless Veterans program: expansion of eligibility; extension of authorization

- **(a)** *Expansion of eligibility* Section 2002(b)(1) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by inserting “2031,” after “2013,”.
- **(b)** *Extension of authorization* Section 2031(b) of such title is amended by striking “2022” and inserting “2027”.

## § 2. Extension of authorization of program of additional services for homeless veterans

Section 2033(d) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “2022” and inserting “2027”.

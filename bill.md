> To improve recreation opportunities on, and facilitate greater access to, Federal public land, and for other purposes.

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Federal land* The term Federal land means—
  - **(A)** land under the jurisdiction of the Secretary; and
  - **(B)** National Forest System land.
- **(2)** *Indian tribe* The term Indian Tribe has the meaning given the term in section 4 of the Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act (25 U.S.C. 5304).
- **(3)** *Secretaries* The term Secretaries means each of—
  - **(A)** the Secretary; and
  - **(B)** the Secretary of Agriculture.
- **(4)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means the Secretary of the Interior.
- **(5)** *Secretary concerned* The term Secretary concerned means—
  - **(A)** the Secretary, with respect to land under the jurisdiction of the Secretary; or
  - **(B)** the Secretary of Agriculture, with respect to National Forest System land.

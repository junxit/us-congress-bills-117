# S. 5075

> To establish new ZIP Codes for certain Wisconsin communities, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Establishing new ZIP Codes

Not later than 270 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP Code for, as nearly as practicable, each of the following communities:

- **(1)** Village of Somers, Wisconsin.
- **(2)** Village of Mount Pleasant, Wisconsin.
- **(3)** Village of Caledonia, Wisconsin.

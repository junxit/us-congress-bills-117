# S. 3569

> To extend the program to provide liability protections for volunteer practitioners at certain health centers.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Help Expand Liability Protections for Volunteers Act” or the “HELP for Volunteers Act”.

## § 2. Extension of program

Section 224(q) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 233(q)) is amended by striking paragraph (6).

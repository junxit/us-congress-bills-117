# H.R. 6035

> To amend the Consolidated Natural Resources Act of 2008 to extend the authorization of financial assistance with respect to the Abraham Lincoln National Heritage Area.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Abraham Lincoln National Heritage Area Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Abraham Lincoln National Heritage Area

Section 451 of the Consolidated Natural Resources Act of 2008 (Public Law 110–229) is amended by striking “the date that is 15 years after the date of the enactment of this subtitle” and inserting “May 8, 2038”.

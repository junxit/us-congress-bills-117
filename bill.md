# H.R. 9048

> To prohibit a commissioner of the Federal Trade Commission from continuing in office after the expiration of the term of office of the commissioner, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “FTC Commissioner Parity Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition

The first section of the Federal Trade Commission Act (15 U.S.C. 41) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “: Provided, however,” and all that follows through “shall have qualified.”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “and that a commissioner may not continue to serve after the expiration of the term of office for which the commissioner was appointed” after “shall succeed”.

# H.R. 5299

> To amend title 37, United States Code, to authorize travel and transportation allowances for family members to attend the funeral and memorial services of members of the uniformed services.

## § 1. Travel and transportation allowances for family members to attend the funeral and memorial services of members

Section 452(b) of title 37, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new paragraph:

> - **(18)** Presence of family members at the funeral and memorial services of members.

# H.R. 5553

> To prohibit the Secretary of Veterans Affairs from providing or paying for surgical treatment related to gender transition.

## § 1. Prohibition of provision of or payment for surgical treatment related to gender transition by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs

The Secretary of Veterans Affairs may not provide or pay for surgical treatment related to gender transition.

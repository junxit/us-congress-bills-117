# H.R. 7608

> To authorize the Secretary of Health and Human Services to award grants to States to develop, improve, or maintain a State registry of advance directives.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “State Registries for Advance Directives Act of 2022”.

## § 2. Grants for State registries of advance directives

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary of Health and Human Services may award grants to States to develop, improve, or maintain, directly or by contract, a State registry of advance directives.
- **(b)** *Authorization of appropriations* To carry out this section, there is authorized to be appropriated $5,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2023 through 2027.

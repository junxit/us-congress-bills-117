# S. 3338

> To revise the boundary of the Ste. Genevieve National Historical Park in the State of Missouri, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ste. Genevieve National Historical Park Boundary Revision Act”.

## § 2. Ste. Genevieve National Historical Park boundary revision

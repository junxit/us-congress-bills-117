# S. 502

> To amend chapter 53 of title 49, United States Code, to incorporate zero-emission fueling technology into the definition of “capital project”.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “More Access to ZEV Equipment in Transit Act” or the “MAZE in Transit Act”.

## § 2. Zero-emission fueling technology

Section 5302(3)(G)(v) of title 49, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subclauses (XII), (XIII), and (XIV) as subclauses (XIII), (XIV), and (XV), respectively; and
- **(2)** by inserting after subclause (XI) the following:
  > - **(XII)** technology to fuel a zero emission vehicle (as defined in section 5339(c));

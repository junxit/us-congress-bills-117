# H.R. 8414

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the diesel fuel tax.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Relief for our Truckers Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of tax on diesel fuel

- **(a)** *In general* Section 4041(a)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is hereby repealed.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall take effect on the date of the enactment of this Act.

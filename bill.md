# H.R. 4218

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to increase the frequency that the Advisory Committee on Women Veterans shall submit a report to the Secretary of Veterans Affairs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Women Veteran Report Act”.

## § 2. Annual report from the Advisory Committee on Women Veterans

Subsection (c)(1) of section 542 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “even-numbered year” and inserting “year”.

# S. 2880

> To amend the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2020 to modify certain deadlines relating to the Commission on Combating Synthetic Opioid Trafficking.

## § 1. Modification of certain deadlines for Commission on Combating Synthetic Opioid Trafficking

Section 7221(f)(2) of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2020 (Public Law 116–92; 133 Stat. 2273) is amended by striking “270 days” and inserting “390 days”.

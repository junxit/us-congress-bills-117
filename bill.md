# H.R. 9549

> To support the ability of kinship caregivers to provide safety and stability for the children placed in their homes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fostering Secure Family Partnerships Act”.

## § 2. Increase in Federal financial support for kinship navigator programs

- **(a)** *In general* Section 474(a)(7) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 674(a)(7)) is amended by striking “50” and inserting “75”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect on October 1, 2023, and shall apply to payments under part E of title IV of the Social Security Act for calendar quarters beginning on or after such date.

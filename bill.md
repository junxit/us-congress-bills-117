# H.R. 7002

> To authorize the Gateway Arch in St. Louis, Missouri, to be illuminated by blue and yellow lights in support of Ukraine.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Gateway Solidarity Act”.

## § 2. Illumination of the Gateway Arch in support of Ukraine

To show support and solidarity with the Ukrainian people, the Secretary of the Interior shall authorize the Gateway Arch in St. Louis, Missouri, to be illuminated by blue and yellow lights for a certain period of time, to be designated by the Secretary of the Interior.

# H.R. 9422

> To prohibit lending, leveraging, or co-mingling customer funds by cryptocurrency exchanges without consent of a customer.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Crypto Consumer Investor Protection Act”.

## § 2. Lending, leveraging, and co-mingling prohibited

A cryptocurrency exchange may not lend, leverage, or co-mingle the funds of a customer without the consent of such customer.

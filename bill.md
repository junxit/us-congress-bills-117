# S. 2002

> To provide that any termination of a director of a national research institute or national center of the National Institutes of Health be on the basis of malfeasance, neglect of office, or incapacity only.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “National Institutes of Health Director Protection Act”.

## § 2. Removal of directors of the national research institute or national center of the NIH

Section 405(a)(2)(E) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 284(a)(2)(E)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the subparagraph heading, by striking “Rule of construction” and inserting “Termination for cause”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “, except that any such termination may be made only on the basis of malfeasance by, neglect of office by, or incapacity of the director” before the period.

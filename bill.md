# H.R. 7599

> To prohibit the issuance of a passport with any gender designation other than “male” and “female”, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Traditional Passport Act” or the “TPA Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition

The Secretary of State may not issue any passport that contains an “X” gender designation and may permit applicants for passports to select from only “Male” or “Female”.

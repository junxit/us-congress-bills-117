# H.R. 5091

> To establish certain requirements with respect to the appointment of the Attending Physician, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Appointment of Attending Physician

- **(a)** *In general* The Attending Physician shall be appointed by the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the majority leader of the Senate.
- **(b)** *Effective date* Subsection (a) shall apply with respect to an individual appointed to fill a vacancy in the office of Attending Physician after the date of enactment of this Act.

# H.R. 9230

> To increase the authorization of appropriations for grants to States to defray the expenses of the States related to the establishment and operation of a State underground injection control program for permitting Class VI wells for the injection of carbon dioxide.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “State Efforts to Create Underground Regulatory Efficiency Act” or the “SECURE Act”.

## § 2. Authorization for State permitting program grants

Section 40306(c)(3) of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (42 U.S.C. 300h–9(c)(3)) is amended by striking “$50,000,000 for the period of fiscal years 2022 through 2026” and inserting “$100,000,000, which shall be authorized to remain available until expended”.

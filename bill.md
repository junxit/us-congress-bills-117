# H. RES. 1408

> Prohibiting the consideration in the House of Representatives of certain bills or joint resolutions relating to Department of Veterans Affairs mortgage loan fees, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Point of order relating to Department of Veterans Affairs mortgage loan fees

- **(a)** *Point of order* It shall not be in order in the House of Representatives to consider any bill or joint resolution, or amendment thereto or conference report thereon, that—
  - **(1)** makes 1 or more changes in the law with respect to fees collected by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs in connection with a mortgage loan guaranteed under chapter 37 of title 38, United States Code, the budgetary effects of which are savings; and
  - **(2)** contains 1 or more changes in the law or new provisions of law—
    - **(A)** that are not related to—
      - **(i)** veterans housing benefits; or
      - **(ii)** the actuarial soundness of the program for guaranteeing housing loans under such chapter; and
    - **(B)** the budgetary effects of which are costs.
- **(b)** *Definitions* In this section, the terms budgetary effects, costs, and savings have the meanings given those terms in section 3 of the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (2 U.S.C. 932).

# H. RES. 1356

> Of inquiry requesting the President and directing the Attorney General to transmit, respectively, certain documents to the House of Representatives relating to Ray Epps.

That President Joseph R. Biden is requested, and Attorney General Merrick B. Garland are directed, to transmit, respectively, to the House of Representatives, not later than 14 days after the date of the adoption of this resolution, copies of all documents, memoranda, advisory legal opinions, notes from meetings, audio recordings, records (including telephone and electronic mail records), correspondence, and other communications, or any portion of any such communications, to the extent that any such one or more items are within the possession of President Joseph R. Biden or Attorney General Merrick B. Garland, respectively, and refer to Ray Epps.

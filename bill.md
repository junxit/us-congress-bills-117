# S. 5116

> To amend the Child Nutrition Act of 1966 to require the authorization of frozen fruits and vegetables under the special supplemental nutrition program for women, infants, and children.

## § 1. Frozen fruits and vegetables under the special supplemental nutrition program for women, infants, and children

Section 17(f)(11) of the Child Nutrition Act of 1966 (42 U.S.C. 1786(f)(11)) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(D)** *Frozen fruits and vegetables* In the regulations prescribed under subparagraph (A), the Secretary shall require a State agency to make available under the program frozen fruits and frozen vegetables.

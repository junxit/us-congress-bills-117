# H.R. 6887

> To prohibit the owner or operator of a port in the United States to enter into a contract with certain entities for operation or management of such port.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Secure Our Ports Act of 2022”.

## § 2. Prohibition on certain contracts for port operation and management

An owner or operator of a port in the United States may not enter into a contract for the operation or management of such port with an entity that is—

- **(1)** a Chinese, Russian, North Korean, or Iranian state-owned enterprise; or
- **(2)** any foreign entity for which any percentage is owned by a country listed in paragraph (1).

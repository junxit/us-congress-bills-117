# H.R. 6616

> To prohibit the Secretary of the Treasury from requiring the use of facial recognition technology for access to any Internal Revenue Service online account or service.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Save Taxpayers’ Privacy Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on facial recognition for access to Internal Revenue Service accounts

The Secretary of the Treasury may not establish or maintain any verification process for access to an Internal Revenue Service online account or service using facial recognition technology.

# H.R. 4698

> To prevent the use of Federal funds to advance discriminatory concepts, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Say No to Indoctrination Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition

No Federal funds may be appropriated, obligated, or expended to teach or advance concepts that—

- **(1)** separate individuals based on race, color, or national origin;
- **(2)** assign characteristics or assumptions to individuals based on race, color, or national origin; or
- **(3)** state or imply that the United States is an inherently racist country.

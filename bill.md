# H.R. 5062

> To prohibit employment authorization for aliens with final orders of removal, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Americans not Aliens Act”.

## § 2. Prohibiting employment authorization for certain aliens

Section 241(a)(7) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1231(a)(7)) is amended by striking “unless the Attorney General” and all that follows through “to the public interest”.

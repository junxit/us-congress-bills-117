# H.R. 6743

> To amend the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act to increase the reimbursement rate of school meals.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Level Up Nutrition for CHildren in Every School Act of 2022” or the “LUNCHES Act of 2022”.

## § 2. Increasing the Reimbursement Rate of School Meals

Section 4(b)(2) of the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act (42 U.S.C. 1753(b)(2)) is amended by striking “10.5 cents” and inserting “59.5 cents”.

# H.R. 6333

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds to establish, maintain, or support any national database identifying individuals as having received or not received any vaccine.

## § 1. No Federal funds for national database identifying vaccinated or unvaccinated individuals

No Federal funds may be used to establish, maintain, or support any national database identifying individuals as having received or not received any vaccine.

# H.R. 5009

> To prohibit the District of Columbia Government from requiring individuals to present documentation certifying COVID–19 vaccination as a condition of entering any building, facility, or other venue in the District of Columbia.

## § 1. Prohibiting District of Columbia Government from requiring certification of COVID–19 vaccination as condition of entering buildings and facilities in District of Columbia

The District of Columbia Government may not require an individual to present documentation certifying the individual’s COVID–19 vaccination as a condition of entering any building, facility, or other venue, including an outdoor venue, in the District of Columbia.

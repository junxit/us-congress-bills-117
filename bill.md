# S. 3393

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to permanently extend the Health Coverage Tax Credit.

## § 1. Permanent extension of Health Coverage Tax Credit

- **(a)** *In general* Subparagraph (B) of section 35(b)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “, and before January 1, 2022”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to coverage months beginning after December 31, 2021.

# H.R. 6613

> To extend certain national school lunch program requirement waivers addressing COVID–19, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Keeping School Meals Flexible Act”.

## § 2. Extension of certain national school lunch program requirement waivers addressing COVID–19

Section 2202 of the Families First Coronavirus Response Act (42 U.S.C. 1760 note) is amended—

- **(1)** by amending subsection (e) to read as follows:
  > - **(e)** *Sunset* The authority of the Secretary to establish, grant, or extend a waiver under this section shall expire on June 30, 2023.

- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(g)** *Authorization of appropriations* There is authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out this section.

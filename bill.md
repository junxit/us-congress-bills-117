# H.R. 3034

> To amend title 28, United States Code, to provide an additional place for holding court for the Western District of Washington, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Additional place for holding court for the Western District of Washington

Section 128(b) of title 28, United States Code, is amended by inserting “Mount Vernon,” after “Tacoma,”.

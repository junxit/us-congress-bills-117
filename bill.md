# S. RES. 422

> Authorizing the use of the atrium in the Philip A. Hart Senate Office Building for a bipartisan Halloween dog parade on October 27, 2021.

## § 1. Use of the atrium in the Hart Senate Office Building for a bipartisan Halloween dog parade

The atrium in the Philip A. Hart Senate Office Building is authorized to be used on October 27, 2021, for a bipartisan Halloween dog parade.

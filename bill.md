# H.R. 8491

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to expand eligible practitioners to furnish telehealth services.

## § 1. Expanding practitioners eligible to furnish telehealth services

Section 1834(m)(4)(E) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395m(m)(4)(E)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “for the 151-day period”; and
- **(2)** by striking “of the period at the end of the emergency sentence” and inserting “of the emergency period”.

# H.R. 7408

> To require the Securities and Exchange Commission to update the Commissions’ guidance on economic analysis in rulemakings, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “SEC Economic Analysis Modernization Act”.

## § 2. Guidance on economic analysis for SEC rulemakings

- **(a)** *In general* Not later than 2 years after the date of enactment of this Act, and every 5 years thereafter, the Securities and Exchange Commission shall update the Commission’s guidance on economic analysis in rulemakings.
- **(b)** *Availability of update* Upon the completion of each update required under subsection (a), the Commission shall make such update available to the public, including on the website of the Commission, along with a summary and justification of any changes made by the update.

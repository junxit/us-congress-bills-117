# H.R. 9547

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 120 Doty Street in Kaukauna, Wisconsin, as the “Sgt. Nickolas Mueller Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Sgt. Nickolas Mueller Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 120 Doty Street in Kaukauna, Wisconsin, shall be known and designated as the “Sgt. Nickolas Mueller Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Sgt. Nickolas Mueller Post Office Building”.

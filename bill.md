# H.R. 6864

> To amend title 46, United States Code, to repeal certain antitrust exemptions for ocean common carriers.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ocean Shipping Antitrust Enforcement Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of antitrust exemptions

- **(a)** *Repeal* Section 40307 of title 46, United States Code, is repealed.
- **(b)** *Clerical amendment* The item relating to section 40307 in the analysis for chapter 403 of title 46, United States Code, is repealed.
- **(c)** *Conforming amendment* Section 40301(e) of such title is amended by striking “(except sections 40305 and 40307(a))” and inserting “(except section 40305)”.

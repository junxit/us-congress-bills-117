# H.R. 4298

> To clarify the disparate burden test, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Inclusive Elections Act of 2021”.

## § 2. Disparate burden test

Section 2 of the Voting Rights Act of 1965 (52 U.S.C. 10301) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(c)** In determining whether members of a class of citizens have less opportunity than other members of the electorate to participate in the political process and to elect representatives of their choice, a court shall consider whether—
>   - **(1)** the challenged standard, practice, or procedure imposes a disparate burden on members of a class of citizens protected by subsection (a); and
>   - **(2)** the disparate burden is in part caused by or related to social and historical conditions that produce or produced discrimination against members of the protected class.

# H.R. 3210

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1905 15th Street in Boulder, Colorado, as the “Officer Eric H. Talley Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Officer Eric H. Talley Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1905 15th Street in Boulder, Colorado, shall be known and designated as the “Officer Eric H. Talley Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Officer Eric H. Talley Post Office Building”.

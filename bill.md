# S. 4266

> To amend the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 to increase funding for stormwater management for Atlanta, Georgia, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “City of Atlanta Stormwater Management Act”.

## § 2. Stormwater management, Atlanta, Georgia

Section 219(e)(5) of the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 (106 Stat. 4835; 110 Stat. 3757; 113 Stat. 334) is amended by striking “$25,000,000” and inserting “$75,000,000”.

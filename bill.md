# S. RES. 129

> Notifying the President of the United States of the election of a Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate.

That the President of the United States be notified of the election of the Honorable Karen H. Gibson as Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate.

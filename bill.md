# H.R. 4988

> To amend the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 to establish that reconciliation directives in a budget resolution may not cause a net increase in the deficit for the period of fiscal years covered by that resolution.

## § 1. Increase in deficit prohibited under reconciliation directives

Section 310(a) of the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (3) by striking “or” at the end;
- **(2)** in paragraph (4) by striking the period at the end and inserting “; or”; and
- **(3)** by adding the following new paragraph at the end:
  > - **(5)** not specify in paragraphs (1) and (2), when taken together, amounts that would have the effect of increasing the deficit for the period of fiscal years covered by such resolution.

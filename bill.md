# H.R. 9436

> To codify the Department of Labor rule regarding religious exemptions to the equal opportunity clause, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Codifying the Department of Labor rule regarding religious exemptions to the equal opportunity clause

The rule issued by the Secretary of Labor on December 9, 2020, relating to “Implementing Legal Requirements Regarding the Equal Opportunity Clause’s Religious Exemption” (85 Fed. Reg. 79324) shall have the force and effect of law.

# H.R. 266

> To name the Air Force Utah Test and Training Range as the “Bishop Utah Test and Training Range”, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Name of Air Force Utah Test and Training Range

The Air Force Utah Test and Training Range shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the “Bishop Utah Test and Training Range”. Any reference to such test and training range in any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall be considered to be a reference to the Bishop Utah Test and Training Range.

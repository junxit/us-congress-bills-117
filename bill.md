# H.R. 8702

> To prohibit the implementation of the proposed rule entitled “Nondiscrimination in Health Programs and Activities”.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Taxpayers Support Life Act”.

## § 2. Prohibiting implementation of proposed rule entitled “Nondiscrimination in Health Programs and Activities”

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary of Health and Human Services may not finalize, implement, administer, or enforce the proposed rule entitled “Nondiscrimination in Health Programs and Activities” (RIN: 0945–AA17).
- **(b)** *Limitations on funding* No Federal funds may be used—
  - **(1)** to finalize, implement, administer, or enforce the proposed rule described in subsection (a); or
  - **(2)** in contravention of administering or enforcing the final rule entitled “Nondiscrimination in Health and Health Education Programs or Activities, Delegation of Authority” (85 Fed. Reg. 37160) and dated June 19, 2020.

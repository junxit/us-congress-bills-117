# S. 2734

> To require Senate confirmation of the Director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Restoring Trust in Public Health Act”.

## § 2. Appointment of the Director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention

Part A of title III of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 241 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> ## § 310B. Appointment of the Director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
>
> The President shall appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, the Director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention.

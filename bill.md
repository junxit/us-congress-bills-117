# H.R. 7612

> To advance desalination research and technological innovation, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Desalination Research Advancement Act”.

## § 2. Desalination research authorization

- **(a)** Section 8 of the Water Desalination Act of 1996 (42 U.S.C. 10301 note; Public Law 104–298) is amended in subsection (a)—
  - **(1)** by striking “$5,000,000 per year for fiscal years 1997 through 2021” and inserting “$20,000,000 per year for fiscal years 2022 through 2026”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “$1,000,000” and inserting “$15,000,000”.
- **(b)** Section 3(e) of the Water Desalination Act of 1996 is amended—
  - **(1)** in paragraph (5), by striking “and”;
  - **(2)** in paragraph (6), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
    > - **(7)** to minimize the impacts of seawater desalination on aquatic life and coastal ecosystems, including technologies to monitor and reduce those impacts.

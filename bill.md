# H.R. 9294

> To amend the Claims Resolution Act to make a technical correction to the Taos Pueblo Water Development Fund and the Aamodt Settlement Pueblos’ Fund, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Authorization of payment of adjusted interest on the Taos Pueblo Water Development Fund

Section 509(c)(1) of the Claims Resolution Act (Public Law 111–291) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(C)** *Adjusted interest payments* In addition to the amounts made available under subparagraphs (A) and (B), there is authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary for deposit into the Taos Pueblo Water Development Fund $7,794,297.52.

## § 2. Authorization of payment of adjusted interest on the Aamodt Settlement Pueblos’ fund

Section 617(c)(1) of the Claims Resolution Act (Public Law 111–291) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(C)** *Adjusted interest payments* In addition to amounts made available subparagraphs (A) and (B), there is authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary $4,314,709.18 to further assist the Pueblos in accordance with subparagraph (B).

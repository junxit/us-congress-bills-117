# S. 3413

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the credit for health insurance costs of eligible individuals.

## § 1. Extension of Health Coverage Tax Credit

- **(a)** *In general* Subparagraph (B) of section 35(b)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “January 1, 2022” and inserting “January 1, 2026”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to coverage months beginning after December 31, 2021.

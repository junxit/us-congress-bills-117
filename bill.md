# H.R. 4981

> To amend the Fentanyl Sanctions Act, to modify certain deadlines relating to the Commission on Combating Synthetic Opioid Trafficking.

## § 1. Modification of certain deadlines for Commission on Combating Synthetic Opioid Trafficking

Section 7221(f)(2) of the Fentanyl Sanctions Act (133 Stat. 2273) is amended by striking “270 days” and inserting “390 days”.

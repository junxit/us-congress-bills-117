# H.R. 7075

> To designate Ukrainian Independence Park in Washington, District of Columbia, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ukrainian Independence Park Act of 2022”.

## § 2. Designation of Ukrainian Independence Park

- **(a)** *Designation*
  - **(1)** *In general* The area described in paragraph (2) shall be designated as “Ukrainian Independence Park”.
  - **(2)** *Description of area* The area designated under paragraph (1) is the approximately 0.35 acres generally depicted as “Ukrainian Independence Park” on the map entitled “Ukrainian Independence Park Proposed Boundary”, numbered 802/180,561, and dated June 2022.
- **(b)** *Reference* Any reference in any law, regulation, document, record, map, paper, or other record of the United States to the area or properties described in subsection (a) is deemed to be a reference to “Ukrainian Independence Park”.
- **(c)** *Signage* The Secretary of the Interior may post signs on or near Ukrainian Independence Park that include information on the importance of Ukraine’s independence, freedom, and sovereignty and the solidarity between the people of Ukraine and the United States.

# S. 4378

> To reauthorize a provision of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act pertaining to drugs containing single enantiomers.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Hatch-Waxman Improvement Act of 2022”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of provision pertaining to drugs containing single enantiomers

Section 505(u) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 355(u)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1)(A)(ii)(II), by adding “(other than bioavailability studies)” after “any clinical investigations”; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (4), by striking “October 1, 2022” and inserting “October 1, 2027”.

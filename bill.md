# H.R. 5895

> To prohibit payments from the Judgment Fund to aliens who are not lawful permanent residents, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ending Payments to Non-American Citizens Act”.

## § 2. Limitation on judgment fund payments to aliens

No funds from the Judgment Fund may be made available to an alien who is not a lawful permanent resident, unless specifically authorized by law.

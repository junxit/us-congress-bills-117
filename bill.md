# S. RES. 494

> Memorializing the unborn by lowering the United States flag to half-staff on the 22nd day of January each year.

## Preamble

- Whereas, on January 22, 1973, the majority of the members of the Supreme Court of the United States ruled that abortion was a right secured by the Constitution; and
- Whereas, since that fateful day, over 60,000,000 unborn children have perished: Now, therefore, be it

That the Senate—

- **(1)** supports the recognition of the Day of Tears in the United States on the 22nd day of January each year; and
- **(2)** encourages the people of the United States to lower their flags to half-staff to mourn and honor the innocents who have lost their lives to abortion.

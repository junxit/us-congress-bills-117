# S. 4299

> To repeal the sunset for collateral requirements for Small Business Administration disaster loans.

## § 1. Repeal of sunset for collateral requirements for SBA disaster loans

Section 2102(b) of the RISE After Disaster Act of 2015 (15 U.S.C. 636 note) is repealed.

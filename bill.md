# H.R. 8039

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to expand the period during which days of service on active duty or of performance of active service reduce the age of eligibility for members of the Ready Reserve for retired or retainer pay, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Reduction in age of eligibility of members of Ready Reserve for retired or retainer pay based on certain service

- **(a)** *In general* Section 12731(f)(2)(A) of title 10, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “January 28, 2008, the eligibility” and inserting “September 11, 2001, the eligibility”;
  - **(2)** by striking “in any fiscal year after January 28, 2008, or”; and
  - **(3)** by striking “September 30, 2014” and inserting “September 11, 2001”.
- **(b)** *Applicability* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall apply to applications pending on or submitted on or after the date of the enactment of this Act.

# H.R. 6609

> To prohibit the Secretary of the Treasury from using facial recognition technology to access any Internal Revenue Service online account.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Facial Authorization Cannot be Enforced Act” or the “FACE Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on facial recognition for access to Internal Revenue Service accounts

The Secretary of the Treasury may not establish or maintain any verification process for access to an Internal Revenue Service online account using facial recognition technology.

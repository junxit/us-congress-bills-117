# S. 3006

> To amend the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985 to extend the discretionary spending limits for fiscal years 2022 through 2031.

## § 1. Extension of discretionary spending limits

Section 251(c) of the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985 (2 U.S.C. 901(c)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (7)(B), by striking “and” at the end; and
- **(2)** by inserting after paragraph (8) the following:

  >   - **(A)** for the revised security category, $684,930,000,000 in new budget authority; and

# H.R. 8382

> To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to require an alien arriving from a foreign territory contiguous to the United States to return to such territory, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Migrant Protection Protocols Act of 2022”.

## § 2. Treatment of aliens arriving from contiguous territory

Section 235(b)(2)(C) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1225(b)(2)(C)) is amended by striking “may return” and inserting “shall return”.

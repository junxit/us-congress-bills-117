# H.R. 5281

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to require reporting on vulnerabilities due to climate change and cyberattacks in the National Technology and Industrial Base, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Securing Technology Against Climate Impacts Act”.

## § 2. Inclusion of information regarding climate change in reports on National Technology and Industrial Base

Section 2504(3)(B) of title 10, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating clauses (i) through (iii) as clauses (ii) through (iv), respectively; and
- **(2)** by inserting before clause (ii), as so redesignated, the following new clause (i):
  > - **(i)** vulnerabilities related to the current and projected impacts of climate change and to cyberattacks or disruptions;

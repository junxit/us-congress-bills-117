# H.R. 2350

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal chapter 35 (relating to taxes on wagering).

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Discriminatory Gaming Tax Repeal Act of 2021”.

## § 2. Repeal

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 35 of subtitle D of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is repealed.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2020.

# H.R. 5372

> To prohibit the Department of Health and Human Services from using any Federal funds to create or maintain an office to address climate and health equity, or for any climate change program, project, or activity.

## § 1. Prohibition against HHS climate change activities

No Federal funds may be used by the Department of Health and Human Services—

- **(1)** to create or maintain an office to address climate and health equity; or
- **(2)** for any climate change program, project, or activity.

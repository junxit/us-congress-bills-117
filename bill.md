# H.R. 6981

> To authorize the President to waive export criteria and transportation and related costs for transportation of certain ammunition and ordnance to supply entities providing ammunition to Ukraine, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “American Ammo in Defense of Ukraine Act”.

## § 2. Waiver of export criteria and transportation and related costs for transportation of certain ammunition and ordnance to Ukraine

- **(a)** *In general* In the case of ammunition or ordnance with Export Classification Number 0A501 or Category 3 on the U.S. munitions list that is to be delivered to a confirmed military logistical supply entity providing ammunition to Ukraine, as designated by the Secretary of Defense, the President may—
  - **(1)** provide for the transportation of the ammunition or ordnance without charge to Ukraine for the costs of such transportation; and
  - **(2)** waive any applicable export criteria of the Department of State or the Department of Commerce to expedite the delivery of the ammunition or ordnance.
- **(b)** *Termination* The authority under subsection (a) shall terminate when the armed conflict in Ukraine ends.

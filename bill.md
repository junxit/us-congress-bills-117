# H.R. 2408

> To extend the authorization for Arabia Mountain National Heritage Area.

## § 1. Authorization extended for Arabia Mountain national heritage area

Section 240 the National Heritage Areas Act of 2006 (Public Law 109–338; 16 U.S.C. 461 note) is amended by striking “terminates on” and all that follows through the final period and inserting “October 12, 2036”.

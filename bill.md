# H.R. 1971

> To extend the authority of the Secretary of the Interior to provide assistance to the local coordinating entity for the Atchafalaya National Heritage Area under subtitle B of Public Law 109–338.

## § 1. Atchafalaya National Heritage Area

Notwithstanding section 221 of subtitle B of Public Law 109–338, the authority of the Secretary of the Interior to provide assistance to the local coordinating entity for the Atchafalaya National Heritage Area shall terminate on the date that is 30 years after the date of the enactment of Public Law 109–338.

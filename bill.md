# H.R. 8225

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to improve the treatment of anesthesiologist assistants in the Veterans Health Administration of the Department of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Anesthesiologist assistants in Veterans Health Administration

- **(a)** *Appointments of anesthesiologist assistants* Section 7401(1) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by inserting “anesthesiologist assistants,” after “physician assistants,”.
- **(b)** *Additional pay for anesthesiologist assistants* Section 7454(a) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by inserting “, anesthesiologist assistants,” after “Physician assistants”.

# H.R. 5835

> To prohibit the use of Federal funds to establish, implement, or enforce any vaccine mandate.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Defund Federal Vaccine Mandates Act”.

## § 2. No Federal funds for any vaccine mandate

No Federal funds may be used to establish, implement, or enforce any vaccine mandate.

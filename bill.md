# S. 1377

> To extend the effective date for the limitation on colocation and administration of veterans educational assistance State approving agencies, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Extension of effective date for limitation on colocation and administration of veterans educational assistance State approving agencies

Section 1024(b) of the Johnny Isakson and David P. Roe, M.D. Veterans Health Care and Benefits Improvement Act of 2020 (Public Law 116–315) is amended by striking “on the date that is 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act” and inserting “on September 30, 2022”.

# S. RES. 834

> Adjusting the special reserve percentage available to committees of the Senate.

## § 1. Special reserve adjustment

Section 20(a)(3) of Senate Resolution 70 (117th Congress), agreed to February 24, 2021, is amended by striking “7 percent” and inserting “11 percent”.

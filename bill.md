# S. 1641

> To prohibit rescinding the recognition of Israel's sovereignty over the Golan Heights.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Israel Sovereignty Reassurance Act of 2021” or “ISRA”.

## § 2. Prohibition on rescinding the recognition of Israel's sovereignty over the Golan Heights

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, none of the funds appropriated or otherwise made available for the Department of State for fiscal year 2022 may be used to prepare, propose, draft, review, or promulgate any regulation, guidance, or executive order or to otherwise implement, administer, or enforce any policy that rescinds the recognition by the United States that the Golan Heights are a part of Israel.

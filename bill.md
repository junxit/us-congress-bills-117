# H.R. 6172

> To direct the United States Postal Service to designate a single, unique ZIP Code for Kinnelon, New Jersey.

## § 1. Single, unique ZIP Code for Kinnelon, New Jersey

Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this section, the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP Code applicable to the area encompassing solely the municipal boundaries of the Borough of Kinnelon, New Jersey.

# S. 4958

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend the authority for financial assistance for supportive services for very low-income veteran families in permanent housing, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Keeping Our Commitment to Ending Veteran Homelessness Act of 2022”.

## § 2. Extension of authority for financial assistance for supportive services for very low-income veteran families in permanent housing

Section 2044(e)(1)(H) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “2021 and 2022” and inserting “2021 through 2024”.

## § 3. Extension of program for homeless services at additional locations

Section 2033(d) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “September 30, 2022” and inserting “September 30, 2024”.

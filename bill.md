# H.R. 3254

> To terminate Federal Pandemic Unemployment Compensation.

## § 1. Termination of Federal Pandemic Unemployment Compensation

- **(a)** *In general* Section 2104(e)(2) of the CARES Act (15 U.S.C. 9023(e)(2)) is amended by striking “September 6, 2021” and inserting “May 31, 2021”.
- **(b)** *Amount* Section 2104(b)(3)(A)(ii) of such Act (15 U.S.C. 9023(b)(3)(A)(ii)) is amended by striking “September 6, 2021” and inserting “May 31, 2021”.

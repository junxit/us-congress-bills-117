# H. CON. RES. 123

> Providing for a correction in the enrollment of H.R. 1437.

That, in the enrollment of H.R. 1437, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall amend the title so as to read: “Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2023, and for other purposes.”.

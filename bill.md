# H.R. 4153

> To advance clean power technology development and use through innovation and clean energy standards, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Clean Energy Future Through Innovation Act of 2021”.

## § 2. Definitions; table of contents

- **(a)** *Definitions* In this Act:
  - **(1)** *Commission* The term Commission means the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission.
  - **(2)** *Secretary* The term Secretary means the Secretary of Energy.

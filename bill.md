# H.R. 9123

> To direct the United States Postal Service to designate a new ZIP Code for certain communities in Wisconsin.

## § 1. Designating new ZIP Code

Not later than 270 days after the enactment of this Act, the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP Code for, as nearly as practicable, each of the following communities:

- **(1)** Village of Somers, Wisconsin.
- **(2)** Village of Mount Pleasant, Wisconsin.
- **(3)** Village of Caledonia, Wisconsin.

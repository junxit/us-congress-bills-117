# H.R. 6676

> To amend title 18, United States Code, to prohibit administering COVID–19 vaccines to minors without parental consent, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting Parental Rights Act”.

## § 2. Administering COVID–19 vaccines to minors without parental consent

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 7 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > ## § 120. Administering COVID–19 vaccines to minors without parental consent
  >
  > Whoever, in or affecting interstate or foreign commerce, administers a COVID–19 vaccine to an individual who has not attained the age of 18 without the prior consent of a custodial parent or legal guardian of that individual, shall be fined under this title, imprisoned not more than 1 year, or both.

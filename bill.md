# S. 5179

> To amend the Endangered Species Act of 1973 to require congressional approval of certain actions, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Congressional approval of certain actions

Section 4(a) of the Endangered Species Act of 1973 (16 U.S.C. 1533(a)) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(4)** A listing, removal from a list, or change in status of any species under this subsection shall take effect upon enactment of a congressional joint resolution of approval.

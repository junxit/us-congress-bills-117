# H.R. 6690

> To prohibit the Secretary of Transportation from issuing any rule, regulation, or guidance that promotes the use of speed safety cameras, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Prohibition on rule promoting use of speed safety cameras

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Secretary of Transportation may not propose, issue, or implement any rule, regulation, or guidance that promotes the use of speed safety cameras.

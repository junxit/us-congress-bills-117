# S. 5291

> To delay the implementation of the modifications of exceptions for reporting of third party network transactions.

## § 1. Delay in modification of exceptions for reporting of third party network transactions

Section 9674(c)(1) of the American Rescue Plan of 2021 is amended by striking “December 31, 2021” and inserting “December 31, 2022”.

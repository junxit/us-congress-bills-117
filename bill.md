# H.R. 8589

> To prohibit the Securities and Exchange Commission from finalizing the proposed rule titled “The Enhancement and Standardization of Climate-Related Disclosures for Investors”, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Prohibition on rulemaking

The Securities and Exchange Commission may not finalize the proposed rule titled “The Enhancement and Standardization of Climate-Related Disclosures for Investors” (87 Fed. Reg. 21334; published April 11, 2022) or implement or enforce such a finalized rule or any substantially similar rule.

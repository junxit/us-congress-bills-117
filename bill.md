# H.R. 8633

> To amend the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 with respect to stormwater management for the city of East Point, Georgia, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “City of East Point Stormwater Management Act”.

## § 2. East Point, Georgia, stormwater management

Section 219(f)(136) of the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 (106 Stat. 4835; 113 Stat. 336; 121 Stat. 1261) is amended by striking “$5,000,000 for” and inserting “$15,000,000 for stormwater management and other”.

# H.R. 7015

> To provide for the conveyance of seized Russian and Belarusian assets to the people of Ukraine.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Confiscating Corrupt Criminal Proceeds (CCCP) Act of 2022”.

## § 2. Use of certain forfeited assets

Section 981(e) of title 18, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (6), by striking “or” at the end;
- **(2)** in paragraph (7), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; or”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(8)** in the case of any property owned by a Russian or Belarusian national, to the Administrator of the United States Agency for International Development, to be made available to provide humanitarian assistance to the people of Ukraine.

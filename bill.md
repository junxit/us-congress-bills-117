# H.R. 9194

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to impose an excise tax on water use during droughts, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Domestic Water Protection Act of 2022”.

## § 2. Excise tax on growth of certain water-intensive crops

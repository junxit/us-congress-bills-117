# H.R. 4887

> To amend section 1603 of title 28, United States Code, to except the People’s Republic of China as a defined foreign state.

## § 1. Exception

Section 1603(b)(2) of title 28, United States Code, is amended by inserting “except the People’s Republic of China,” after “owned by a foreign state,”.

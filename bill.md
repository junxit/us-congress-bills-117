# H.R. 6840

> To prohibit COVID–19 travel restrictions with respect to any nationals of Canada, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Northern Border Reopening Act”.

## § 2. Prohibiting COVID–19 travel restrictions for Canadian nationals

Notwithstanding Presidential Proclamation 10294 (86 Fed. Reg. 59603), published October 28, 2021, relating to advancing the safe resumption of global travel during the COVID–19 pandemic, or any other provision of law, neither a suspension nor limitation on entry to the United States shall apply to a national of Canada based on any COVID–19 travel restriction.

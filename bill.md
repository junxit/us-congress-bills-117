# H.R. 5025

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to increase and extend the authorization of appropriations for homeless veterans reintegration programs.

## § 1. Increase and extension of authorization of appropriations for homeless veterans reintegration programs

Section 2021(e)(1)(F) of title 38, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “$50,000,000” and inserting “$75,000,000”; and
- **(2)** by striking “2022” and inserting “2025”.

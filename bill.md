# H.R. 6278

> To amend the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act to make a violation of such Act, relating to the enforcement of a storage lien, a felony.

## § 1. Increased penalties for enforcement of a storage lien in violation of the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act

Section 307(c) of the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (50 U.S.C. 3958(c)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the heading, by striking “Misdemeanor” and inserting “Criminal penalties”;
- **(2)** by striking all that follows “so” and inserting an em dash; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following new paragraphs:
  > - **(1)** shall be fined as provided in title 18, or imprisoned for not more than one year, or both; or
  > - **(2)** after a prior conviction under this section has become final, shall be fined as provided in title 18, or imprisoned for not more than three years, or both.

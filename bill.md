# H.R. 8067

> To make technical changes clarifying section 503 of the Federal Credit Reform Act of 1990.

## § 1. Technical changes with respect to cost estimates

Section 503(d) of the Federal Credit Reform Act of 1990 (2 U.S.C. 661b(d)) is amended by striking the period at the end and inserting “, which shall include the percent guaranteed on loan guarantee commitments.”.

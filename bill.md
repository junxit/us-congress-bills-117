# S. 5183

> To amend the Federal Credit Union Act to provide a sunset for certain ways in which credit unions may be Agent members of the National Credit Union Administration Central Liquidity Facility.

## § 1. Agent membership

- **(a)** *In general* Section 304(b)(2) of the Federal Credit Union Act (12 U.S.C. 1795c(b)(2)) is amended by striking “all those credit unions” and inserting “such credit unions as the Board may in its discretion determine”.
- **(b)** *Sunset*
  - **(1)** *In general* Section 304(b)(2) of the Federal Credit Union Act (12 U.S.C. 1795c(b)(2)) is amended by striking “such credit unions as the Board may in its discretion determine” and inserting “all those credit unions”.
  - **(2)** *Effective date* The amendments made by paragraph (1) shall take effect on December 31, 2027.

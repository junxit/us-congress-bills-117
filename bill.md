# H.R. 7765

> To amend the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946 to direct the Secretary of Agriculture to establish Supply Chain Regional Resource Centers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “American Food Supply Chain Resiliency Act of 2022” .

## § 2. Supply Chain Regional Resource Centers

The Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946 (7 U.S.C. 1621 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

## § 3. Agriculture Innovation Centers Program

Section 6402(a) of the Farm Security and Rural Investment Act of 2002 (7 U.S.C. 1632b(a)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (2), by striking “and” at the end;
- **(2)** in paragraph (3), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(4)** research, technical assistance, support services, outreach, and other services to strengthen, maintain, and secure supply chains related to agricultural inputs and agricultural products produced in the United States.

# H.R. 3261

> To repeal the Authorization for Use of Military Force Against Iraq Resolution.

## § 1. Repeal of Authorization for Use of Military Force Against Iraq Resolution

The Authorization for Use of Military Force Against Iraq Resolution (Public Law 102–1; 50 U.S.C. 1541 note) is hereby repealed.

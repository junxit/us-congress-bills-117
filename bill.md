# H.R. 9608

> To amend the Federal Water Pollution Control Act to make certain activities for the removal of plastic waste and post-consumer materials from wastewater eligible for assistance under the State water pollution control revolving funds, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Reducing Plastics in Wastewater Act”.

## § 2. Removal of plastic waste and post-consumer materials

- **(a)** *Projects and activities eligible for assistance* Section 603(c) of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1383(c)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in paragraph (11)(B), by striking “; and” and inserting a semicolon;
  - **(2)** in paragraph (12)(B), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(13)** for the construction of improvements to treatment works owned and operated by a municipality to reduce and remove plastic waste and post-consumer materials, including microplastics and microfibers, from wastewater.

- **(b)** *Additional subsidization* Section 603(i)(1) of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1383(i)(1)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (A)(ii)(III), by striking “; or” and inserting a semicolon;
  - **(2)** in subparagraph (B)(iv), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; or”; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(C)** for an activity described in subsection (c)(13).

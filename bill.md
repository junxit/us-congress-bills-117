# S. 3019

> To require the United States Postal Service to designate a single, unique ZIP code for particular communities.

## § 1. ZIP Codes

Not later than 270 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the United States Postal Service shall designate a single, unique ZIP code for, as nearly as practicable, each of the following communities:

- **(1)** Ocoee, Florida.
- **(2)** Miami Lakes, Florida.
- **(3)** Oakland, Florida.
- **(4)** Village of Estero, Florida.

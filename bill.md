# H.R. 7996

> To require congressional authorization for the drawdown and sale of petroleum products in the Strategic Petroleum Reserve, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Strategic Petroleum Reserve Anti-Exploitation Act”.

## § 2. Drawdown and sale of petroleum products

Section 161 of the Energy Policy and Conservation Act (42 U.S.C. 6241) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking subsection (d) and inserting the following:
  > - **(d)** *Congressional authorization required* The Secretary may not draw down and sell petroleum products in the Reserve unless the drawdown and sale is authorized by an Act of Congress or joint resolution in response to a national emergency.

- **(2)** by repealing subsection (h).

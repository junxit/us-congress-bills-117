# H. RES. 1127

> Honoring our Nation’s fallen law enforcement officers for National Police Week.

## Preamble

- Whereas police officers protect and serve our Nation’s communities;
- Whereas many brave men and women, deserving of honor, and with the last full measure of devotion, gave their lives in service of the Thin Blue Line;
- Whereas these fallen heroes deserve the highest respect;
- Whereas, in 2021, 73 police officers were killed in the line of duty protecting their respective communities, including 5 police officers from the State of Louisiana;
- Whereas National Police Week is from May 11 until May 17 for 2022; and
- Whereas National Peace Officers Memorial Day is May 15: Now, therefore, be it

That the House of Representatives honors our Nation’s fallen law enforcement officers for National Police Week.

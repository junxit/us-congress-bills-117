# H.R. 6994

> To prohibit United States Government recognition of the Russian Federation’s claim of sovereignty over any part of Ukraine (to include Crimea), its airspace, or its territorial waters, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ukraine Sovereignty Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition against United States recognition of the Russian Federation’s claim of sovereignty

- **(a)** *Statement of policy* It is the policy of the United States not to recognize the Russian Federation’s claim of sovereignty over any part of Ukraine (to include Crimea), its airspace, or its territorial waters.
- **(b)** *Prohibition* In accordance with subsection (a), no Federal department or agency may take any action or extend any assistance that implies recognition of—
  - **(1)** the Russian Federation’s claim of sovereignty over any part of Ukraine (to include Crimea), its airspace, or its territorial waters; and
  - **(2)** the claims of sovereignty of the “Donetsk People’s Republic”, the “Luhansk People’s Republic”, or any other closely related successor entities.

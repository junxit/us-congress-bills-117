# H.R. 8999

> To amend the Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970 to provide for the expiration of emergency temporary standards after 6 months.

## § 1. Expiration of emergency temporary standards

Section 6(c)(2) of the Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970 (29 U.S.C. 655(c)(2)) is amended by inserting before the period at the end the following: “, but in no case shall such emergency temporary standard remain in effect for a period exceeding 6 months”.

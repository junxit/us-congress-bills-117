# H.R. 6768

> To amend the Middle Class Tax Relief and Job Creation Act of 2012 to reauthorize the First Responder Network Authority.

## § 1. Reauthorization of First Responder Network Authority

Section 6206 of the Middle Class Tax Relief and Job Creation Act of 2012 (47 U.S.C. 1426) is amended by striking subsections (f) and (g).

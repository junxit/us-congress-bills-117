# H.R. 8849

> To prohibit the Corps of Engineers from issuing a permit for the Delta Conveyance Project, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop Delta Tunnels Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on Delta Conveyance Project permit

The Secretary of the Army, acting through the Chief of Engineers, may not issue a permit under section 404 of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1344) relating to the Delta Conveyance Project referred to in the document published by the California Department of Water Resources on January 15, 2020, entitled “Notice of Preparation of Environmental Impact Report for the Delta Conveyance Project”.

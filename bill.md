# S. 4565

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to repeal the copayment requirement for recipients of Department of Veterans Affairs payments or allowances for beneficiary travel, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Repeal of copayment requirement for recipients of Department of Veterans Affairs payments or allowances for beneficiary travel

Section 111 of title 38, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking subsection (c);
- **(2)** by redesignating subsections (d) through (g) as subsections (c) through (f), respectively; and
- **(3)** in subsection (b)(1), in the matter before subparagraph (A), by striking “Except as provided in subsection (c) of this section and notwithstanding subsection (g)(2) of this section” and inserting “Notwithstanding subsection (f)(2)”.
